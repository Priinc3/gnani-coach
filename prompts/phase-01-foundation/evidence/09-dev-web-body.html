Speaking Coach

Practice speaking, not just studying.

A calm conversation studio for live spoken practice: interview answers, workplace exchanges, everyday situations and travel small talk, with short coaching breaks between rounds.

Foundation build for an adults-only pilot. Everything on this page runs locally: no provider is connected, the microphone stays off, and nothing is stored.

What is real in this build

  • Real: this setup screen, the shared draft v0.1 contracts, and a local voice-service skeleton whose health check proves the process starts.
  • Real: your microphone is never requested, and no connection, transcript, waveform or score is simulated.
  • Not built yet: live speech providers, recording, audio analysis, accounts and saved history. Sign-in and saved history are planned, not working.
